Why Brokers Prefer Not to Share Commercial Real Estate Data on Transactions Commercial real estate is well known for being an industry where information doesn’t flow freely. Very few brokers are willing to share commercial real estate data publicly, especially “closed” data. Closed data is related to transactions or the sale of commercial real estate.
